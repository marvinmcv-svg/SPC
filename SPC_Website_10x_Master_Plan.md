# SPC — Website 10x Master Plan

**Client:** Specialty Polymer Coatings, Inc. (SPC) — https://www.spc-net.com/
**Document type:** Strategy, brand profile, competitor research, build plan and master build prompt
**Status:** Plan only (nothing built yet). Items marked ⚠️ VERIFY need confirming with SPC before launch.

## 0. Executive summary

SPC makes high-performance coatings that protect pipelines, tanks, ships and water infrastructure. These products sit on assets worth billions of dollars. The current website reads like an online brochure from about 2015. It has a thin homepage, no clear value proposition and no email address. The contact page is empty, there is no live chat or WhatsApp, the locations page lists one office, there are no case studies, and navigation is confusing.

**The 10x goal:** turn spc-net.com into SPC's **best salesperson and best technical-service rep, working 24/7**. It should:

1. **Answer** engineers, specifiers, applicators and procurement teams instantly, through an AI receptionist trained on SPC's product data sheets and approvals.
2. **Convert** visitors to qualified leads (RFQs, spec requests, sample requests and technical calls) through WhatsApp, chat, call booking and smart forms.
3. **Prove** performance with case studies, approvals, test data, project maps and video.
4. **Equip** the field with a spec builder, coverage and volume calculators, a datasheet hub, application guides and offline mobile access.
5. **Rank** for every high-intent coatings search, such as "girth weld coating", "100% solids epoxy pipeline", "potable water tank lining" and "Lloyd's approved marine epoxy", and get cited by AI answer engines like ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews.

## 5. Signature features

### 5.1 🤖 AI receptionist: "SPC Tech Assistant"

**Role:** a 24/7, multilingual front desk and first-line technical-service rep on the website and WhatsApp.

**Capabilities:**
- **Answers technical questions** using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) over every PDS, SDS, application guide, approval certificate, FAQ and case study, **always citing the source document** with a download link.
- **Recommends a coating system** from a short guided conversation covering asset, substrate, service temperature, immersion, exposure and application method. It hands the result to the spec builder.
- **Qualifies leads** by capturing name, company, role, country, project type, quantity and timeline. It scores the lead and routes it to the right regional sales rep or distributor by territory.
- **Books meetings** in the rep's calendar (Google or Microsoft 365 via Cal.com or Calendly).
- **Generates quotes requests** (RFQs) prefilled into the CRM.
- **Takes photos:** a user uploads a picture of a coating defect and gets AI-assisted triage (pinholes, holidays, sags, amine blush), always with "a technician will confirm".
- **Speaks the user's language**, auto-detected (EN/ES/FR/PT/AR and more).
- **Voice mode** (phase 2): an AI phone receptionist on the main number for after-hours calls, with transcripts sent to the CRM.
- **Hands off to a human:** a "talk to a person" button at any time, with live takeover by SPC staff in a shared inbox and full context passed along.

**Guardrails:**
- It never invents specs, approvals or prices. If something isn't in the knowledge base, it says so and escalates.
- It quotes no pricing, only routes to sales.
- It always includes a safety disclaimer for application advice and links the SDS.
- PII handling meets GDPR and PIPEDA, with consent captured before saving contact details.
- Every conversation is logged for QA. A weekly review of unanswered questions feeds new FAQ content.

**Tech:** Claude (latest Sonnet model for speed and cost, Opus for complex technical reasoning) via the Anthropic API, a vector store (Supabase pgvector), a document ingestion pipeline for PDFs (with OCR), tool calling for `search_docs`, `recommend_system`, `create_lead`, `book_meeting`, `handoff_human` and `get_distributor`, and a streaming chat UI.

### 5.2 💬 WhatsApp widget and WhatsApp Business

- A **floating WhatsApp button** on every page (bottom-right, stacked with the AI chat launcher) plus "Chat on WhatsApp" CTAs on contact, product and case-study pages.
- A **click-to-chat deep link** (`https://wa.me/<SPC_NUMBER>?text=...`) prefilled with the page context, for example: *"Hi SPC, I'm looking at SP-2888 RG and have a question about…"*
- A **QR code** on desktop (scan to continue on your phone).
- **Business hours awareness**: "Typically replies in X minutes" or "Our AI assistant will reply now".
- **WhatsApp Business Platform (Cloud API)** integration so the **same AI receptionist answers on WhatsApp**: datasheet PDFs sent in-chat, photo triage, lead capture, and handoff to a human agent in a shared inbox (Chatwoot, Respond.io or Twilio).
- Regional numbers (⚠️ VERIFY): Canada, USA, Australia, plus distributors where they agree.
- Analytics: track WhatsApp clicks as conversions in GA4 and the CRM.
- Opt-in for template messages such as order and shipment updates and webinar reminders.

### 5.3 Interactive system selector ("click the asset")
An illustrated or 3D scene of a pipeline right-of-way, tank farm, vessel and water treatment plant. Clicking a zone (for example a girth weld, tank floor or ballast tank) shows the recommended SPC system with DFT, cure, approvals, datasheets, a "Build spec" button and an "Ask the AI" button.

### 5.4 Spec builder
A step-by-step wizard covering asset, substrate, surface prep, service conditions, standards and application method. The output is a branded **spec package** (PDF, editable DOCX spec text and a ZIP of PDS and SDS), emailed to the user and logged as a lead.

### 5.5 Calculators
- **Volume and kit estimator** (upgrade of the existing tool): pipe OD × number of joints × cutback width × DFT gives litres and kits, then a one-click RFQ.
- **Coverage calculator** for tanks and areas
- **Cure-time calculator** by substrate temperature and humidity
- **VOC and carbon savings** of 100% solids against a solvent-borne equivalent, for ESG reports

### 5.6 Proof engine
- **Case-study templates:** challenge, solution, product, numbers, photos and video, client quote
- **Global projects map** (Mapbox), filterable by market, product and year
- **Approvals and certificates wall** with downloadable certificates and expiry dates
- **Client logos** where permitted

### 5.7 Resources hub
Faceted search across all documents by product, language, document type and region. A **document basket**: add several PDS and SDS files and email or download them as a ZIP (the Carboline app pattern, but on the web). Versioned documents with "last updated" dates.

### 5.8 Distributor and applicator portal (phase 3)
Login for distributors and certified applicators. Includes price lists, marketing assets, order and shipment status, training records and certification badges, and co-branded spec sheets.

### 5.9 Training and certification
Applicator training course booking, with online modules (video plus quiz) leading to a **certified SPC applicator** badge and a public "find a certified applicator" directory.

## 7. SEO and AEO (AI answer engine) strategy

- **Clean URLs** such as `/products/sp-2888-rg/` instead of `?prod=`, with 301 redirects mapped from every old URL.
- **Structured data (JSON-LD):** Organization (with parentOrganization RPM), Product (with additionalProperty for specs), Article, FAQPage, LocalBusiness for each office, BreadcrumbList, VideoObject.
- **Programmatic pages**: product × market × standard, for example "girth weld coating CSA Z245.30" or "NSF 61 potable water tank lining". Only build these where the content is genuinely useful.
- **Topic clusters:** field-joint coating, liquid epoxy versus FBE, 100% solids explained, holiday testing, cathodic disbondment, amine blush, DFT measurement, coating in cold weather.
- **AEO:** concise answer-first paragraphs, FAQ blocks, cited data, `llms.txt`, and fast server-rendered HTML so ChatGPT, Perplexity and Google AI Overviews quote SPC.
- **International SEO:** hreflang tags and localized pages, not just translated ones.
- **Core Web Vitals targets:** LCP < 2.0s, INP < 200ms, CLS < 0.05, Lighthouse 95+ in all categories.
